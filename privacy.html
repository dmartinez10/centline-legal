Privacy policy

Draft 2, updated 2026-11-06

Who we are

Centline is an app for owner operators and small carriers, made by Centline LLC, a Iowa limited liability company ("we"). In the App Store and Google Play, Centline is published by David Guillermo Martinez for Centline LLC. This policy says what Centline keeps, why, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. Questions go to support@centline.co.

What Centline keeps

Your account: your email address and your password. The password is stored scrambled by our database provider; we never see it. If you turn on a code from an authenticator app, the secret for it is kept too.

Your name, if you or your owner type it.

Your business: its name, MC number and base state, its trucks, and who drives for it.

Your trips: odometer readings, the states you cross, the days and times you type, deliveries, deadhead and the notes you add.

Your money: money in and money out, with the category, amount, broker, load number and who was paid.

Receipt photos you take or pick, and the numbers saved from them. A receipt can show part of a card number, a name or an address, so treat it as private.

The weekly trip records you send and the IFTA quarters you file, kept as they were sent.

A record of changes: who changed what and when, so a record can be trusted in an audit.

What stays on your phone

Centline keeps a copy of your entries on your phone so it works with no signal, and sends them when it has signal again.

App lock uses Face ID or your passcode through your phone. Centline never receives your face or your passcode.

What Centline does not do

Centline does not use GPS or track where your phone is. The states on a trip are the ones you enter.

No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, no selling or renting your information, and no analytics tools.

Who else handles your information

Supabase runs our database, sign in and file storage. Everything above is stored there. Receipt photos are in private storage and open only through links that expire in minutes.

Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, reads receipt photos, but only if you say Yes when Centline asks. Centline sends the photo of the receipt and Claude reads the date, total, gallons and state. You check every number before it is saved. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic may not train its models on what we send. Anthropic says it deletes what it receives within 30 days, unless it must keep it longer to enforce its usage policy or by law. You can change your answer at any time in Settings, under "Centline reads receipts". With No, you type the numbers yourself and no photo is sent to Anthropic.

Sentry receives crash reports so we can fix what breaks: the phone model, its system version, the app version and where in the app the crash happened. Before a report leaves your phone, Centline removes email addresses, links and long numbers. Crash reports are not tied to your account.

Each of these companies handles your information only to provide its service to us, under its own terms, which protect it at least as well as this policy.

Who in your business sees what

A business only ever sees its own information.

An owner sees everything in the business, including the trips, miles, fuel and fuel receipts of drivers in it.

A driver sees his own trips and his own money. A driver's other money is not shown to the owner; his fuel is, because the business needs it for IFTA.

When your information is shared

Only with the companies named above, when you send a weekly trip record or other file yourself to someone you choose, or when the law requires it. If we are ever required by law to hand over your information, we will tell you unless the law forbids it.

How long it is kept

Your business's information is kept until the owner deletes the business. Deleting a receipt in the app hides it, but the photo is kept for an audit until the business is deleted.

IFTA asks carriers to keep their records for 4 years. Save copies of anything you may need before deleting a business.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Settings, Delete my account. You can also write to support@centline.co from the email address you sign in with.

When an owner deletes his account, every business he alone owns closes at once. For 7 days it is read only: he and his drivers can still make their file, and he can undo it by signing in. Then everything in it is deleted: trips, money, receipt photos, records and the sign ins of everyone in it. Copies we keep are gone within 30 days.

A driver in a deleted business keeps his sign in if he belongs to another business. If not, his sign in is deleted too.

When a driver deletes his account, his sign in and his name are deleted. The trips and fuel he entered stay with the business, because they are the business's IFTA records. His name stays on weekly trip records already sent, because a sent record is kept as it was sent.

More at https://dmartinez10.github.io/centline-legal/delete.html.

Your choices

You can change the Centline answer, turn app lock on or off, and sign out on every phone, all in Settings.

You can ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by writing to support@centline.co. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Each business is locked off from every other one in our database, and every change is checked on our server. No system is perfect; if your information is ever exposed, we will tell you as the law requires.

Age

Centline is for adults running a trucking business. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date and version at the top change, and Centline tells you in the app before a change that matters takes effect.
