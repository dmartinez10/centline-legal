Delete your Centline account

Draft 2, updated 2026-11-06

How to delete

In the app: Settings, Delete my account. Type DELETE and your password, then tap Delete my account, then Yes.

Or write to support@centline.co from the email address you sign in with, with "Delete my account" in the subject. We write back to check it is you.

An owner

Every business you alone own closes at once. For 7 days it is read only: you and your drivers can still make your file.

Changed your mind? Sign in within the 7 days and tap Keep Example LLC, with your business's name. After that, everything in the business is deleted: trips, money, receipt photos, records and the sign ins of everyone in it. Copies we keep are gone within 30 days.

A driver who also drives for another business keeps his sign in. If not, his sign in is deleted too.

A driver

Your sign in and your name are deleted. The trips and fuel you entered stay with the business, because they are its IFTA records.

Your name stays on weekly trip records already sent, because a sent record is kept as it was sent.

Before you delete

IFTA asks carriers to keep their records for 4 years. Make your file first: Settings, Export my data. It holds your trips, money, receipt photos, trip records and IFTA quarters. Once deleted, they cannot be brought back.
