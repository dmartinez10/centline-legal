Delete your Centline account

Draft 1, updated 2026-11-05

How to ask

Write to support@centline.co from the email address you sign in with. Put "Delete my account" in the subject, and say whether you are the owner deleting the business or a driver deleting your own account.

We write back to check it is you, then delete within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

An owner deleting the business: everything in it, trips, money, receipt photos, records and the accounts of everyone in it, including any copies we keep.

A driver deleting his account: his sign in and his name. The trips and fuel he entered stay with the business, because they are its IFTA records.

Before you delete

IFTA asks carriers to keep their records for 4 years. Save copies of your weekly trip records, IFTA summaries and receipts first. Once deleted, they cannot be brought back.
